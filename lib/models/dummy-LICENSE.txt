Model notes:
  - Full-body adult male skeleton + superficial/deep muscles (634 named meshes).
  - Vertex colors: red muscle fibers, pale tendon/fascia, ivory bone.
  - Units: metres, Y-up, figure faces +Z.
  - Gap vs preferred hero: Cmdr. Elena Reyes is female-presenting; no CC
    female full-body muscular GLB of comparable quality was available quickly.
    This male CC BY-SA ecorché is the closest openly licensed full-body muscle
    model used for the interactive 3D hero. A female still (generated original)
    is kept only as a non-interactive style reference, not as the rotatable hero.
